# Bender — changelog

What changed for players, version by version.

## 0.1.0 — current

- First version. Two clock benders, A and B. Each glides the tempo from the
  clock's to the clock × mult and back over a number of beats, with its own
  curves and balance, then pauses (rest or hold). Loop or one-shot, a
  shared reset. Taps: the two bent clocks and a rate CV for each (1 V/oct).
  The audio passes through untouched.
